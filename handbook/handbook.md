# Wag & Whisker Store Handbook

*Wag & Whisker is a fictional store created for an AI engineering project. All products, people and policies are invented.*

This handbook is the single reference for how Wag & Whisker works: who we are, how we keep toys safe, every product we sell, and the policies that apply to orders, shipping, returns, warranty claims, recalls and our loyalty programme. If a question isn't answered here, please contact us using the details in Section 10.

## 1. About Wag & Whisker

Wag & Whisker was founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon by Maya Torres. The idea started with her rescue dog, Biscuit, a cheerful mixed breed with a talent for destroying cheap toys in minutes. After a year of sweeping stuffing off the floor and fishing plastic squeakers out of Biscuit's mouth, Maya decided to make toys she could trust: toys made from honest materials, sized properly, and built to last as long as the animal playing with them.

What began as a handful of rope toys sold at weekend markets is now an online store with a catalogue for dogs, cats and small pets. Every order is packed and shipped from our warehouse in Portland. We don't use third-party sellers or overseas fulfilment centres, which means we can check every parcel before it leaves.

Our values are simple, and they guide every product decision:

- **Durable.** A toy that falls apart is not only a waste of money, it can be dangerous. We design for the way animals actually play, not the way we wish they did.
- **Non-toxic.** Pets chew, lick and carry their toys. We only use materials we would be comfortable seeing in a child's mouth.
- **Honest sizing.** We give clear size and weight guidance for every toy, and we would rather tell you a toy isn't right for your pet than sell you the wrong one.

The team is small: Maya leads product design, a warehouse crew of six packs every order, and our customer care team answers questions by email and phone. We read every review and every message, and many of the toys in this handbook were changed because a customer told us how their pet really used them.

### How a toy gets made

Every new toy starts with a problem a real pet has. Maya sketches a design, the team builds a handful of prototypes by hand, and the prototypes go home with staff and volunteer families for a month of real play. We watch how dogs grip a toy, where cats lose interest, and which parts small pets chew first. Only designs that survive that month go on to independent safety testing (see Section 2), and only designs that pass testing reach the catalogue. Most ideas never make it that far, and we're comfortable with that.

### Packaging and sustainability

Our toys ship in plain recycled cardboard boxes sealed with paper tape. We don't use plastic air pillows or polystyrene; instead, we cushion parcels with shredded cardboard from our own warehouse offcuts. Product tags are printed on recycled card and attached with cotton string. Wherever possible, we buy materials from suppliers in North America to keep transport distances short.

### Who we design for

We design for three groups of animals: dogs of every size and chewing style, cats from kittenhood onwards, and small pets such as rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters and gerbils. We don't currently make toys for birds, reptiles or fish, because we don't yet have the expertise to design for them safely.

## 2. Safety Standards

Safety is the reason Wag & Whisker exists, so we hold every product to the same rules before it reaches our catalogue.

### Materials policy

We build our toys from a short list of materials: natural rubber, food-grade silicone, organic cotton and felted wool, plus untreated natural woods and grasses for our small-pet range. We do not use BPA, phthalates or latex fillers in any product. Dyes, where we use them, are water-based and colour-fast. If a material isn't on our approved list, it doesn't go into a Wag & Whisker toy.

### Independent testing

Every design is independently tested to ASTM F963 before it goes on sale. Testing covers small parts, sharp edges, seam strength and the materials themselves. When we change a design, even slightly, it is tested again.

### Chew ratings explained

Every dog toy carries a chew rating from 1 to 4 so you can match the toy to how your dog plays:

- **1 Gentle:** for licking, carrying and soft mouthing. Ideal for older dogs, gentle players and enrichment toys.
- **2 Moderate:** for light chewing and tugging. Most dogs who play nicely fall here.
- **3 Strong:** for determined chewers who work at a toy until something gives.
- **4 Power:** our "Built Tough" line, for dogs that destroy most toys. These toys use our densest rubber and thickest walls.

Cat and small-pet toys don't carry chew ratings, because cats and small pets play in very different ways. Instead, each product lists the animals and ages it suits.

### Supervision

No toy is indestructible. Always supervise play, check toys regularly, and replace any toy that shows cracks, loose threads, missing pieces or deep tooth marks. Remove a toy straight away if a piece comes off.

### Choosing the right toy

The right toy depends on three things: your pet's size, how they play, and how much supervision you can give.

For dogs, start with the chew rating. If your dog has destroyed toys before, look at ratings 3 and 4. If your dog mostly carries toys around or plays gently, ratings 1 and 2 will feel more satisfying and are kinder on older teeth. Then check the size and weight guidance on the product itself in Section 3. A toy that is too small for a large dog can become a choking hazard, even if the material is tough.

For cats, think about the kind of play your cat enjoys. Wand and teaser toys suit cats who like to stalk and pounce with you, while kickers, mice and track toys suit cats who play on their own. Many cats enjoy a mix, so rotating toys every few days keeps them interesting.

For small pets, look for toys that are safe to chew, because rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters and gerbils chew almost everything. Our small-pet range uses natural, untreated woods and grasses for this reason.

### Caring for toys

Clean toys regularly, especially toys used for treats or played with outdoors. Each product in Section 3 lists any specific care instructions. As a general rule, rubber and silicone toys can be washed in warm soapy water, fabric and rope toys should be dried completely before they go back into play, and wooden or hay toys shouldn't be soaked. Inspect toys whenever you clean them, and retire any toy that is starting to break apart.

### Rotating toys

Pets get bored of the same toy, just like people do. Keeping two or three toys out at a time and swapping them every few days makes each toy feel new again and spreads the wear across more toys, so each one lasts longer.

## 4. Ordering and Payment

You can place an order on our website at any time. You'll receive an order confirmation email straight away, and a second email with tracking details when your parcel ships.

### Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payment is taken when you place your order.

### Currency

All prices and charges are in US dollars only, including orders shipped outside the United States. If your card is in another currency, your bank will convert the amount at its own rate.

### Changing or cancelling an order

An order can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of being placed. Changes include switching a size, adding or removing items, or correcting the delivery address. After 2 hours, the order is packed and can't be changed. To request a change, email us with your order number as soon as possible.

## 5. Shipping

All orders ship from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon.

### Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm Pacific on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm Pacific, or on a weekend or US federal holiday, ship the next business day.

### United States: standard shipping

Standard shipping within the US takes 3–5 business days after the order ships and costs $5.95. Standard shipping is free on orders of $35 or more.

### United States: express shipping

Express shipping within the US takes 1–2 business days after the order ships and costs $14.95. Express shipping isn't available to PO boxes.

### International shipping

We ship internationally only to Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia. International delivery takes 7–14 business days after the order ships and costs a flat $19.95 per order. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer on delivery and aren't included in our prices or shipping charges.

### Tracking

Every order includes tracking. Your tracking link is in the shipping confirmation email.

### Delivery problems

If tracking shows your parcel as delivered but you can't find it, check with neighbours and around your property first, as carriers sometimes leave parcels in a sheltered spot. If it still hasn't turned up, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date shown in tracking and we'll open an investigation with the carrier.

If your parcel is delayed well beyond the delivery times above, contact us with your order number and we'll check its progress with the carrier for you.

### Incorrect addresses

Please double-check your delivery address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, the address can be corrected only while the order can still be changed (see Section 4). Once a parcel has shipped, we can't redirect it.

## 6. Returns and Exchanges

We want every toy to be the right fit. If something isn't right, here's how returns and exchanges work.

### Return window

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery.

### Used toys

For hygiene reasons, toys that have been used or played with can't be returned unless they are defective. If a toy is defective, see Section 7.

### How to start a return

Start a return from the link in your order email. Choose the items you're returning, then print the return label and attach it to the parcel.

### Return shipping cost

A $6 return label fee is deducted from your refund, unless the item is defective.

### Refunds

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the refund.

### Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size of the same product are free, with no return label fee.

### What happens after you send a return

When your return arrives, our warehouse team checks that the item is unused and in its original packaging. If everything is in order, we process the refund (see the timing above) and send you a confirmation email. If an item doesn't meet the return conditions, we'll email you to explain and send the item back to you at no charge.

### Items bought with Pack Points

If you return an item from an order where you redeemed Pack Points, the points are returned to your account and any amount you paid by card or other method is refunded as normal.

## 7. Damaged, Defective and Warranty

### Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or turns out to be defective, report it within 7 days of delivery and include a photo of the problem. We'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.

### The Built Tough Guarantee

Products with chew rating 4, the Iron Jaw Bone and the Ultra Chew Ring, carry our Built Tough Guarantee. If your dog destroys one within 90 days of delivery, we'll replace it free of charge. The guarantee covers one replacement per product.

## 8. Recalls and Safety Notices

### Squeaky Sam recall (March 2024)

In March 2024 we recalled the "Squeaky Sam" plush toy (former SKU WW-D-099) because its squeaker could detach during play. Squeaky Sam has been discontinued. If you still own one, stop using it and email us for a full refund; no receipt is needed.

### How we share safety notices

Current safety notices are posted on our website and emailed to every customer who bought the affected product.
